01

Scope

Problem statement

Repeat blindness: a recurring issue stops registering because it has become part of the background. REO named this before the pilot began.

"They're all rushing to get their job done and overlooking things that they see every single day. It is a behavioral change and it has to start at the top."

Learning & Development Manager, Citizens REO
Source: REO pilot intake and discovery calls, March and April 2026.

Practice selection

  • Three practices were scoped for REO. Fresh Eyes was sequenced first.
  • Deliberate observation is a prerequisite for the other two: coaching a team, and vendor accountability.
  • Branch visits are learner-initiated and recur weekly. No added time or travel.
  • REO directed the pilot at managers before frontline colleagues.
Source: Practice ranking, REO scoping documents, April 2026. Practice sequence confirmed with Citizens April 14, 2026.

Method

Delivery

One activity per week

Each activity attaches to a branch visit already on the participant's schedule. Target under 10 minutes in the tool per week.

Coaching

AI coach during the activity

The coach requests specifics, the location, the customer impact, the owner and the target date, and does not close until the finding is documented.

Output

A record in FAMIS 360

Findings are logged in 360 against the location, with an owner and a target date. Nothing is simulated.

Measurement

Baseline and follow-up surveys

Participants rate themselves on five behaviors. Managers rate each direct report on three. Both scales run 0 to 10.

Activity tiers

Basic

Fresh Eyes Walkthrough

Ten to fifteen minutes on a scheduled branch visit. Parking lot, entrance, lobby, teller line, restrooms. Document at least one finding.

Result

42 completions. Highest-volume activity in the library.

Intermediate

Catch the Repetitive Issue

Review 360 before the visit. Find a work order open past 60 days or an issue logged more than once. Categorize why it persisted.

Result

39 completions.

Advanced

End-to-End Quality Walk

Twenty-five to thirty-five minutes. Full customer path. Rank findings by what a first-time customer would notice. Act on the top item.

Result

23 completions, plus 25 of the Complete Customer-Eye Audit.

Activities are scaled by difficulty from 1 to 10. One activity tests transfer: complete the work order you came for, then document a finding unrelated to it. 43 of 48 attempts produced an unrelated documented finding.

Twelve examples

Root cause

Repairs applied to the wrong roof

Multiple work orders had been raised to repair the vestibule roof. The leak recurred and damaged the drywall ceiling. Water was entering through a gap on the main roof.

"There have been multiple work orders submitted to repair the small roof above the entry way but the water leak still happens... we found that the small roof was in good shape and not the cause."
WhitehouseFM Lead · Jun 3
Root cause

Water pressure open over a year

Pre-visit 360 review surfaced water pressure work orders recurring for more than twelve months. Categorized as a vendor gap. General contractor redispatched with a target date of July 31.

"Issues has been happening for over a year. Redispatching my GC. With it being water pressure it could still be the City responsibility but they keep saying the pressure is fine on their end."
DearbornFacility Manager · Jun 4
Root cause

Tickets closed on one sensor reading

Temperature control issue recurring since April. On site the participant found the control center was closing tickets on BMS readings alone. Logged an action step naming who must change the closure standard.

"The control center often closes out the ticket using readings from the BMS system, which is only one metric. Not all avenues are explored or nuances investigated."
WoodmereSenior FM · Jun 12
Root cause

Drive-thru closed by recurring leaks

Multiple roofing tickets had produced multiple repairs. Leaks continued and the drive-thru was closed. Escalated the same day to the Senior Project Manager for a structural assessment.

"We have submitted multiple roofing tickets in which roofers have come out and make repairs but we still expereince leaks."
Airport PlazaFacility Manager · Jul 13
Vendor

Breaker open since May 1

Pre-visit 360 review at a substituted location returned one item: an electrical circuit breaker replacement open since May 1. The general contractor had not seen it in their queue.

"Open since 5/1/26. The GC had missed seeing this in their queue. TheY are scheduling electrician the week of 6/22."
HermitageFacility Manager · Jun 22
Vendor

Front doors open past 60 days

Replacement stalled on materials, not vendor performance. Maintenance tech adjusted the doors on the visit. Contractor committed to an end-of-July delivery. Branch, regional manager and supervisor notified.

"I had my maintenance tech adjust the doors so they work temporarily and are safe until the materials are delivered and the job can be completed. I called the contractor and the doors are coming in at the end of July."
BethelFM Lead · Jun 11
Vendor

Mulching deferred by the vendor

Annual mulching had not been performed. The landscaping team's stated reason was the season. Site inspection contradicted it. Assessment logged with a photo, an action step and a target date.

"The landscaping team says 'it is too early to mulch.' Mulch beds most definitely need freshening up."
BurgettstownFacility Manager · Jun 30
Vendor

ATM cleaning cadence changed

Two janitorial work orders in 90 days for the ATM face. Cause categorized as cleaning frequency against usage on a 24-hour vestibule ATM. Standing routine changed, not a new ticket.

"I called my janitorial tech and asked him to wipe down the face of the ATM more often during the busy season... He agreed and will start each time he cleans the branch."
Peters TownshipFM Lead · Jun 11
Safety

Louver ranked above cosmetic finding

Three findings at a recently renovated branch: a missing lamp-post globe, a loose gable-vent louver, an exposed janitor sink. Ranked the louver first and escalated to the retail FM.

"The globe is a bit ugly, but the louver could fall presenting a safety issue, so I will go with the louver is the top priority."
Washington St, Middletown CTFacility Manager · Jul 16
Safety

Cable across the teller line

The drive-thru card machine was in use at the teller line with its cable stretched across the floor. Hazard stopped during the visit. IT ticket recommended to relocate the port.

"I addressed it with the staff on the spot and told them even though it may be an inconvenience they will need to walk the card over for now. I suggested they put in a IT ticket to have the port moved to the teller line."
FlourtownFacility Manager · Jul 15
Outside scope

Abandoned dumpster explained a ticket

On site for a landscaping review with an open trash-accumulation work order flagged in advance. Found a dumpster left by the former hauler. Cause recorded against the recurring ticket.

"We logged an old dumpster left onsite by the former trash hauler. This was also the reason for the trash accumulation around the dumpster reported in 360."
LimerickSenior FM · May 20
Outside scope

Shared-lot ownership determined

A parking lot repair had stalled because ownership of an adjoining lot was undetermined. Participant established the area was the landlord's responsibility. Repair scheduled that week.

"I have found out that the area within the parking lot that is need of repair is the Landlord's responsibility. I have contacted the Landlord and they are making the repair this week."

Program map

Four business problems Flint and REO have discussed for Real Estate Operations, each holding several initiatives. A program is a business problem that cuts across REO. An initiative is one performance gap, run over roughly three months, with a defined audience. Fresh Eyes is one initiative inside Customer Focus. Everything else on this map is at idea stage.
