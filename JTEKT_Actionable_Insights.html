Build Trust & Credibility on the FloorHow Flint reads what leaders write

Feedback becomes insight

After every activity, JTEKT team leaders and supervisors tell the Flint coach what they did and what happened, in their own words. Flint sorts that text into eight categories. First the boxes, then one real conversation, then an illustration of a full initiative.

Every conversation gets two readings: how the work was done, and what came of it. So one conversation can land in more than one box.
