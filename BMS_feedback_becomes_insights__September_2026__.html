AppendixHow the tool reads what associates write

Feedback becomes insight

After every activity, associates describe what they did and what happened — in their own words. Flint reads that text and sorts it into eight categories. The boxes first, then one real conversation from this pilot, then all 300 of them.

Every record gets two readings: how the work was executed, and what came of it. One conversation can therefore land in more than one box — and often does.
