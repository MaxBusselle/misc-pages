Prepared for Tod Brindlinger, Mercury Systems

Operations priorities and the behaviors they require

Mercury has stated what its operations organization is being asked to deliver. Every one of those items depends on what a frontline supervisor does on a given shift.

On appointing Tod Brindlinger, Mercury's chief operating officer Roger Wells said his leadership would be instrumental in ongoing efforts to “eliminate silos in our critical functions,” improve the quality and timeliness of products delivered to customers, and drive operational performance that contributes to financial results.

Roger Wells, Chief Operating Officer, Mercury Systems, June 18, 2024. Company announcement

What every one of those behaviors needs

Practice on the job
Repetition to habit
Manager support
Accountability

A behavior that is trained in a classroom and never practiced on the job does not change output. A behavior practiced once and never repeated does not become a habit. A habit the manager does not support or hold to does not survive a bad week.

Two observations

  • All four behaviors in the right column are supervisor behaviors. None of them are taught in a classroom.
  • Site consolidation and the Phoenix ramp put supervisors in front of new processes and new people at the same time. That is the row where habit and accountability break first.

The question

Of the four behaviors on the right, which one are you least confident in across your sites today?
