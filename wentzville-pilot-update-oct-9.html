Wentzville Pilot Update

October 9, 2026

Improve how team leaders perform the frontline role at Wentzville. Each participant gets short personalized activities in the flow of work, targeted at behaviors that affect the business.

Four business challenges related to the team leader role at Wentzville
  1. Line stops that started as an earlier signal Pilot
  2. Defects created while an operator is behind or new
  3. Jobs run differently than the job instruction sheet
  4. Team leader effectiveness varying with the group leader
The pilot coaches four team leaders in Dave Elliott's area (Trim, IP/Glass, first shift) on challenge 1.

Success criteria

From leadership perspective

  • Activities produce the intended outcome.

  • Failed execution produces usable data.

From participant perspective

  • The right activities for the outcomes.
  • Workable in the flow of work.
  • A good use of their time.
