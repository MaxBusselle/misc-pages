7-Day Pilot Goals

Four questions we will answer together by going through the pilot.

  • 4Questions to answer
  • 7Days
01 · Functionality

Does it work?

You and your users confirm it works, and that it would be worth implementing.

02 · Use cases

How will we use this tool?

We decide together how it fits into your team's day-to-day workflow across teams and the business.

03 · Pricing

How much will it cost?

We give you a clear cost, plus an order form with pricing.

04 · Business case

What is the business case?

We build the case together to show the value is worth it.
