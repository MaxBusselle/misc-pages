The challenge shows up
at every level

The same breakdown repeats itself from the plant office to the floor. Each level passes a version of the problem to the next, and the compounded effect lands on the business.

Challenges by level
LEVEL 01
Plant Managers

Uninformed Decisions

  • Unaware of root causes
  • Reactive and uninformed solutions
  • Plant ends up in an endless "re-training" loop that costs a lot and doesn't improve the problem
LEVEL 02
Supervisors

Don't Pass Info Upwards

Priority focus
  • Not reinforcing QC SOPs (where they exist)
  • Responsible for discipline but not directly overseeing
  • Not coaching TLs to remove operator barriers
LEVEL 03
Team Leads

Don't Address SOP Gaps

Priority focus
  • Lack Quality-Check SOPs
  • Where QC SOPs exist, they're not followed
  • Union and cultural resistance
  • Not supporting operators by asking about, removing, or escalating barriers
  • Not documenting personnel issues for Supervisor discipline
LEVEL 04
Operators

SOPs Not Followed

  • Repeated deviations
  • Inconsistent correction or reinforcement from TLs
  • Barriers to SOP adherence not shared with Leaders
ALSO
Process Engineering

The Process Engineering team doesn't have insight into the improvements needed, so the fixes that would break the cycle never get designed.

Compounded effect

Negative business impacts

Quality escapes, rework and scrap cost, repeated training spend, and a plant that keeps solving the same problem without ever removing its cause.
