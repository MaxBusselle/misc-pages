What this is

Every activity completed during the Fresh Eyes pilot, mapped to the questions on the Quality Control inspection form. The purpose is to let a Quality Control Manager look at the pilot record through the form they already use: pick a question, see the conditions colleagues documented against it between inspections.

290Activities in the record
253Mapped to at least one question
39of 53 questions with at least one finding
175Locations named

How the mapping was produced

Each activity record contains a summary of what the participant did and the participant's own words describing what they found. Those two fields were matched against the wording of each of the 53 inspection questions. A finding matching terms from a question is mapped to it. One finding can map to several questions, because one walk can surface several conditions.

  • 253 of 290 activities mapped to at least one question. 37 did not match any question and are listed as unmapped.
  • 583 question matches in total across 253 findings, a median of 2 questions per finding.
  • Only the participant's own words and the activity summary were matched. The coach's prompts were excluded, because they contain question wording that is not evidence of a condition.

What this mapping is and is not

The mapping is Flint's, produced by term matching, and has not been reviewed by the Quality Control team. It shows which inspection question a documented condition would fall under. It does not show that any inspection missed anything, and it carries no inspection scores, inspection dates, or comparison to any inspection result. Flint holds none of those.

Term matching is approximate. A finding may be mapped to a question that a Quality Control Manager would assign differently, and the 37 unmapped findings may well belong to a question the matching did not catch. Every finding is readable in full below so any mapping can be checked against the underlying record.

Sources: Fresh Eyes initiative activity record, Flint platform, extracted August 25, 2026, 290 completed activities. Inspection Question Matrix by Route of Work, Citizens Real Estate Operations. Participant roles are reported; no participant names appear in this report.

Questions with no pilot findings

14 of the 53 questions have no finding mapped to them. That means the pilot produced no condition matching that question's terms, not that the question is unimportant or that no such condition exists. The ATP measurement questions, 13.01 to 13.04, record a surface test result and have no equivalent in the pilot record at all.

Locations, proposed for the next edition

175 locations were named in the pilot record. Reporting findings by location, against the Quality Control geographic portfolios, would let each Quality Control Manager see the conditions documented at the sites on their own schedule. This section is a concept, not populated data.

ColumnWhat it showsData required
LocationBranch or back-office site name.Held. Named in 175 of 290 activity records.
Quality Control portfolioWhich Quality Control Manager's geographic portfolio the site sits in.Portfolio assignments by site. Not held by Flint.
FindingsCount of pilot findings at the site, and the questions they map to.Held.
Last inspectionDate of the most recent Quality Control inspection at the site.FAMIS 360 inspection export. Not held by Flint.
Repeat sitesSites where the same question was matched more than once across the pilot.Held.

What is needed to populate this

Two items: the Quality Control portfolio assignment for each site, and confirmation that location-level reporting to the Quality Control team is agreed. Location names in the pilot record are as participants typed them and would need normalising against the site list before any counting by location is reliable.
