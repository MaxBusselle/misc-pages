Santa Barbara

About 1 hour 45 minutes from Westwood

Four hotels within a few blocks of each other. Two sit on the waterfront along West Cabrillo Boulevard, facing West Beach and Stearns Wharf. Two are downtown on State Street, the main street of restaurants, bars, and shops. From any of them you can walk to the beach, the harbor, the Funk Zone tasting rooms, and dinner.

Harbor View Inn

Not new: built in the 1980s, last full renovation listed as 2012

The established beachfront choice, and the starting point for this list. Across the street from West Beach and Stearns Wharf, with a pool deck that looks out at the ocean.

The Harbor View Inn is a Spanish Mediterranean style hotel spread across several buildings on West Cabrillo Boulevard, Santa Barbara’s beachfront road. It is a classic, comfortable Santa Barbara stay rather than a design hotel.

What’s good about it. The location is hard to beat. You step out the front door onto the beach path, with Stearns Wharf and the bottom of State Street a few minutes’ walk away. Every room and suite has a private patio or balcony, and many face the water. There is a heated pool overlooking the wharf, a hot tub, a sauna, and a fitness center. It is also the most consistent price on the list: under 400 dollars on every night checked.

What’s nearby. West Beach and the beachfront path, Stearns Wharf, the harbor, the Funk Zone’s wine-tasting rooms and restaurants, and lower State Street, all on foot.

Things to know

It is the least new hotel on this page, and some reviewers describe a mix of updated and dated rooms. The North Wing faces the city and requires crossing a street to reach the front desk. Sources disagree on the room count, from 96 to 149.

The Milo

Renovated: every room redone, renamed June 2025

The Harbor View Inn’s location with rooms redone last year, plus more to do on the property. On the same beachfront strip, a short walk west toward the harbor.

The Milo stands on the site of the Potter Hotel, a 400-room waterfront hotel that opened in 1903 and burned down in 1921. The 2025 renovation, designed by DESIGN360 of Santa Monica, gave every room new furniture, flooring, fabrics, and custom wallpaper, including a heron pattern that nods to the zoo that once stood on the grounds. The look is a relaxed European seaside villa with Spanish Colonial bones.

What’s good about it. It feels more like a small resort than the other Santa Barbara hotels. There are two pools, a hot tub, a bocce court, fire pits, and a small library. Beach umbrellas, chairs, towels, and volleyballs are free to borrow, and cruiser bikes are available to rent. Guests can even borrow professional-grade Sony cameras for free.

What’s nearby. West Beach across the street, the Santa Barbara harbor and Maritime Museum, the beachfront bike path, Stearns Wharf, and the Funk Zone.

Things to know

It is about a mile from the center of State Street, so downtown dinners are a longer walk or a short bike ride. May 1 is the one night above the 400 dollar target. Resort fee and parking cost were not found.

Hotel Santa Barbara

Renovated: all 75 rooms and the lobby, finished August 2025

A 100-year-old downtown landmark, freshly renovated, with a new all-day lobby bar. The pick if you want to walk out the door to dinner and bars.

The hotel has stood at the corner of State and Cota Streets since 1926, the year it reopened after the 1925 earthquake, and it is celebrating its centennial in 2026. The renovation took just over a year and gave every room new furniture, carpet, paint, and updated bathrooms. Reviewers describe the new rooms as moody and comfortable, with deep paint colors and wrought-iron chandeliers, and the lobby as bright, with white arches and columns.

What’s good about it. The new lobby bar, called 1926, is styled after the 1920s and open 7 am to 10 pm, with pastries and fresh juice in the morning and shared plates and cocktails at night. Guests get free bikes, and every floor has filtered and sparkling water stations. Condé Nast Traveler readers included it in their 2026 Readers’ Choice Awards.

What’s nearby. State Street restaurants, bars, and shops within five minutes on foot. The beach and Stearns Wharf are less than a mile away, and the Funk Zone and Amtrak station are a short walk.

Things to know

It is a city hotel, not a resort. The hotel lists a fitness center and meeting space but no pool. Some reviewers mention street noise in the corner rooms. May 1 is above the 400 dollar target.

Drift Santa Barbara

Opened late 2022 after a full restoration

A design hotel in a restored early-1900s building on State Street, across from Hotel Santa Barbara. Minimal and modern inside, with a coffee shop and bar opening onto the street. Books through Marriott.

The building ran as a hotel from 1901 into the 1980s and was one of the few downtown buildings to survive the 1925 earthquake. The Church of Scientology then kept it closed to the public for decades. Local firm Anacapa Architecture restored the Mediterranean exterior, with white stucco, arched windows, and terracotta tile, and built a quiet, minimal interior using wood reclaimed from the original structure.

What’s good about it. It is the most design-focused hotel on this list and the only Santa Barbara option that earns Marriott Bonvoy points. The ground floor has two spots open to the public: Dawn, a coffee shop, and Dusk, a bar serving oysters, ceviche, and cocktails. Garage-style doors open onto the State Street promenade, so it feels lively and local. Local art and ceramics are throughout, and rooms have custom-scented bath products.

What’s nearby. State Street restaurants, bars, galleries, and shops in every direction. The Funk Zone, the beach, and Stearns Wharf are less than a mile away.

Things to know

Rooms are small, about 145 to 185 square feet, with clever storage under the beds. Check-in is keyless, pets are not allowed, and no pool is listed. May 1 jumps to 602 dollars, well above the winter nights.

Laguna Beach

About 1 hour 15 minutes from Westwood, the closest area on the list

An artists’ and surfers’ town with coves, clifftop parks, and a walkable village of galleries and restaurants around Main Beach.

Casa Loma Beach Hotel

Renovated: 15 million dollar renovation unveiled October 2024

The best beach location on the list. A clifftop hotel directly above Laguna’s Main Beach, rebuilt in a relaxed Mallorca style with art everywhere.

Marc and Rose Hospitality, which also runs La Playa Hotel in Carmel, rebuilt the old Inn at Laguna Beach with Electric Bowery, a design firm in Venice, and LAND, an art and branding studio. The design draws on Laguna’s history as a town of artists and surfers. The lobby has a sculpted mural, some rooms have hand-painted tapestries, and a vintage reel-to-reel sound system plays the hotel’s own radio station of coastal music.

What’s good about it. You step out to Main Beach and the village. The Pacific Terrace and pool deck look straight down the coast. Food and drink run like a beach club, open only to hotel guests and Laguna residents: small plates by the pool, spritzes, wine, and agave cocktails. 38 of the 70 rooms have a private balcony or patio, many facing the ocean, and suites come with a record player and a stack of records.

What’s nearby. Main Beach and the boardwalk below the hotel, Heisler Park’s clifftop paths to the north, and the village galleries, shops, and restaurants across the street. Crystal Cove State Park is a five-minute drive.

Things to know

Fees are steep: a 45.60 dollar nightly resort fee plus 70 dollar parking adds about 115 dollars a night. With fees, May 1 comes to about 744 dollars, over the 700 dollar ceiling. Every night is above the 400 dollar target, and breakfast is not included.

Coronado, San Diego

About 2 hours 15 minutes from Westwood

Both hotels are on Orange Avenue, a five-minute walk from each other and from Coronado Beach, one of the widest stretches of sand in Southern California. Downtown San Diego is across the bridge or a short ferry ride.

The Bower Coronado

Opened March 2025, the first new hotel built on Coronado in more than 30 years

Small, quiet, and new, with the only rooftop bar in Coronado. Directly across from the Hotel del Coronado, and under the 400 dollar target on all four nights.

The Bower stands on the site of the Villa Capri, a 15-room hotel that opened in 1956 on the grounds of the old Japanese tea garden of the John D. Spreckels mansion. The new hotel is designed around wabi-sabi, the Japanese idea of natural, understated beauty, using limestone, patinated metal, and raw textures. The original neon Villa Capri sign now hangs behind the rooftop bar.

What’s good about it. Dive, the rooftop bar and restaurant, looks out from Point Loma to Mexico and serves seafood, shared plates, and upgraded dive-bar favorites in a mid-century California setting. Rooms have Stearns and Foster mattresses, Frette robes, Le Labo bath products, and Dyson hair dryers. Each floor has a lounge with house-made pastries and fruit, and bikes and beach chairs are free. It is the first Coronado member of Small Luxury Hotels of the World.

What’s nearby. Coronado Beach and the Hotel del Coronado a five-minute walk away, Orange Avenue’s shops and restaurants, and the ferry to downtown San Diego.

Things to know

With 39 rooms it is a small hotel, and no pool is listed. Valet parking is 65 dollars a night. Even so, it is one of the best values on this page.

Hotel del Coronado

Renovated: six-year, 550 million dollar restoration completed June 2025

The landmark. An 1888 Victorian beach resort right on the sand, freshly restored top to bottom. The splurge option on this list.

The Del is a National Historic Landmark, and it celebrated only the second grand opening in its history on June 18, 2025. The resort has five distinct sections. The original Victorian building has a restored front porch and lobby, 24 stained-glass windows on the front, and rooms redone in a lighter Victorian style with floral fabrics, wicker, and marble bathrooms. Shore House is a new, modern beach-house section with ocean views. Beach Village is a gated group of beachfront cottages and villas. The Cabanas and The Views have refreshed rooms near the pools and activities.

What’s good about it. You are on Coronado Beach, not across the street from it. The restoration added new restaurants, including Serea Coastal Cuisine and a Nobu, plus an Ocean Club, and restored the Victorian garden courtyard and ballrooms. For a once-a-year celebration, this is the most memorable setting on the page.

What’s nearby. Coronado Beach out the back, The Bower and Orange Avenue out the front, and downtown San Diego across the bridge or by ferry.

Things to know

It is the most expensive hotel here: 522 to 560 dollars a night before fees on every date checked. Resort fee and parking cost were not found. It is a large, busy resort, very different from the small hotels on this list, and prices vary a lot by section, so check which building a rate is for.

Palm Springs

About 2 hours from Westwood

December through April is the best season in the desert: warm, dry days and cool nights. Both hotels are close to downtown and the Palm Canyon Drive restaurants, shops, and mid-century architecture.

Casa Palma Hotel & Bungalows

Opened August 2025, the newest hotel on this page

The best value on the list. A 33-room Mediterranean resort on the site of Errol Flynn’s old hotel, with two pools, a tennis court, and free parking.

San Clemente designers Ryan and Rochelle Jaleh rebuilt the property, which Errol Flynn developed in the 1940s as a members-only playground. They took their inspiration from Capri: a bright white exterior draped in bougainvillea, turquoise and gold accents, and Greek-style tiled stairs. They kept the original mid-century facade and the lion head fountains. The grounds are citrus and olive groves and lawns with views of the San Jacinto Mountains.

What’s good about it. It is built for playing and lounging. Two pools are set up for water volleyball and basketball, and there is a full-size tennis court with pickleball lines, fire pits, a barbecue, a fitness center, an on-site market, and free bikes for riding into town. Chiquita, the restaurant, serves Mediterranean food at breakfast, lunch, and dinner, with outdoor seating facing the mountains. Rooms have walk-in tiled showers and Malin and Goetz bath products. Guests rate it 4.8 out of 5 on Google.

What’s nearby. Downtown Palm Springs and Palm Canyon Drive a mile away, the Uptown Design District’s shops and restaurants, and the Palm Springs Art Museum.

Things to know

It is about a mile from downtown, so dinner is a bike ride or short drive. It is under the 400 dollar target on all four nights, and December 12 is the lowest price on the page at 229 dollars. No resort fee was found.

Thompson Palm Springs

Opened October 2024

The big, lively downtown option. A new Hyatt hotel spread over two and a half blocks of Palm Canyon Drive, with a rooftop pool and a mountain-view balcony for every room.

Thompson is Hyatt’s upscale lifestyle brand, and this hotel is the first project between Hyatt and HALL Group, the Napa winery company. With 168 rooms, it is the largest recent hotel opening in Palm Springs. Every room has a balcony facing the San Jacinto Mountains, and the beds have Frette sheets.

What’s good about it. You can walk to everything downtown. The rooftop pool and swim club is the social center. Lola Rose Grand Mezze, a Mediterranean restaurant, is on site, along with a HALL Napa Valley wine-tasting room. Dogs up to 50 pounds stay with no pet fee, and stays earn World of Hyatt points.

What’s nearby. Palm Canyon Drive’s restaurants, bars, and shops right outside, the Thursday night VillageFest street fair, the Palm Springs Art Museum, and the Aerial Tramway a short drive away.

Things to know

It is above the 400 dollar target on all four nights, at 424 to 507 dollars, plus a 49 dollar nightly resort fee. It is busier and more of a scene than the smaller hotels on this page.

Indian Wells and La Quinta

About 2 hours 20 minutes from Westwood

Twenty to thirty minutes past Palm Springs. These are large, recently refreshed golf and spa resorts rather than small boutique hotels. The area is quieter than Palm Springs, with El Paseo shopping in Palm Desert and the Indian Wells Tennis Garden nearby.

Renaissance Esmeralda Resort & Spa

Refreshed 2025 and 2026: new lobby, gastropub, pickleball courts

A full-service resort with golf, tennis, a spa, and freshly redone public spaces. The Marriott option in the desert, and under the 400 dollar target on every night before fees.

The Esmeralda sits at the foot of the Santa Rosa Mountains next to the Indian Wells Golf Resort, with 560 rooms that open onto balconies. The recent updates added a redesigned lobby and reception, Seven Olive, a modern gastropub, new pickleball courts, Topgolf Swing Suites, and the Renew Recovery Lounge, which offers muscle recovery and sleep therapies.

What’s good about it. It has more to do than any other hotel on this page: two championship golf courses next door, tennis and pickleball, Spa Esmeralda, a large pool with cabanas, and four restaurants on site. Stays earn Marriott Bonvoy points, and as a Gold Elite member you may get a room upgrade and late checkout. Pets are welcome for a fee.

What’s nearby. The Indian Wells Golf Resort next door, the Indian Wells Tennis Garden about 10 minutes away, Nobu Indian Wells a 6-minute drive, and El Paseo’s shops and restaurants in Palm Desert.

Things to know

It is a big resort, popular for conferences and families. With the 45 dollar resort fee and parking, the all-in price lands close to 400 dollars rather than under it. You need a car for restaurants off the property.

La Quinta Resort & Club

Renovated for its centennial: rooms and main pool December 2024, lobby and casitas early 2025

The classic old-Hollywood desert resort, nearly 100 years old, with Spanish-style casitas spread across gardens at the foot of the Santa Rosa Mountains.

La Quinta Resort opened in 1926 and became a Hollywood hideaway in its early decades. Most rooms are in low Spanish Colonial casitas set among gardens across about 45 acres, rather than in a tower. The centennial work brightened the rooms, modernized the bathrooms, rebuilt the main pool area, refreshed the lobby, casitas, and villas, and added PLUNGE, an adults-only area at the Renker Pool.

What’s good about it. It feels like a historic estate rather than a big hotel. Spa La Quinta covers 23,000 square feet, and the resort has several restaurants, five bars and lounges, golf, and tennis. It earns Hilton points, not Marriott.

What’s nearby. Old Town La Quinta, a small walkable district of restaurants and shops, less than a mile away. PGA West golf, El Paseo in Palm Desert, and the Indian Wells Tennis Garden are short drives.

Things to know

No rooms showed for December 12 when checked. The other three nights are 378 to 398 dollars, so with the 45.60 dollar resort fee and 15 dollar parking the all-in price is about 440 to 460 dollars. You need a car for most dining off the property.
